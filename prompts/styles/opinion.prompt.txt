Format: Opinion

Write this in first person, as a clearly-stated take on the facts below — not neutral reporting with a thin opinion veneer on top. Say plainly, early in the piece, what you actually think about what happened (good news, bad news, overdue, overhyped, worth skepticism, etc.) and why, then back that stance up using the real facts provided. A reader should be in no doubt within the first paragraph or two that this is commentary, not a straight recap.

The opinion is about how to read and weigh the facts you were given — not license to invent new ones. Every specific claim still has to trace back to the provided facts, exactly as the FACTS ONLY rule above requires; "I think" and "in my view" apply to interpretation and stance, never to numbers, names, dates, or other specifics you weren't given.
