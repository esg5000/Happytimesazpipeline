Nightlife specificity (mandatory):
Phoenix metro nightlife is NOT a club-heavy scene. Do not write nightlife pieces framed around dance clubs, EDM, raves, or hip-hop nightclubs as if that is the dominant format here — it is not. The real Phoenix/Scottsdale nightlife landscape is:
- Old Town Scottsdale is the nightlife hub — a walkable block on and around Scottsdale Road with over 100 bars, lounges, and restaurant-bars. True dance clubs here are rare — maybe 2 or 3. The dominant format is cocktail bars, rooftop lounges, patio bar/eateries, and upscale bar/restaurant hybrids like Olive & Ivy, STK, and Samsara
- Country bars and live music bars are a massive category — Dierks Bentley's Whiskey Row, Rusty Spur Saloon, Scootin Boots Dance Hall, Silver Pony Bar & Grill, Chopper Johns — these are legitimate nightlife destinations here
- Sports bars are a dominant nightlife format given the Cardinals, Suns, Diamondbacks, ASU fan bases — Bottled Blonde doubles as both nightclub and sports bar, Barstool Scottsdale, DraftKings Scottsdale
- Live music venues are a key nightlife category — Crescent Ballroom, Valley Bar, The Van Buren, Last Exit Live, Yucca Tap Room, Marquee Theatre
- Brewery and craft beer nightlife is growing — Four Peaks, Arizona Wilderness, Pedal Haus
- Casino nightlife is significant — Talking Stick Resort, Wild Horse Pass, Fort McDowell
- Mill Avenue in Tempe is the college nightlife strip — Boondocks, Cactus Jacks, Low Key Piano Bar
- Downtown Phoenix has its own emerging scene — Roosevelt Row, Bar Smith, Bitter & Twisted, Cobra Arcade Bar, Century Grand
- Pool parties and rooftop lounges are distinctly Phoenix — W Scottsdale Cottontail, Allegra rooftop, Lylo Swim Club
Never write nightlife pieces framed around generic club culture that does not reflect this market. Always name real Valley venues from the categories above that fit the story angle.
