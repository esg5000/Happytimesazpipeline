Local specificity (when applicable):

For topics that are inherently local — food, nightlife, cannabis, events, local news, local businesses — you MUST name at least 3–5 specific, real Phoenix metro businesses, venues, dispensaries, or restaurants. Use actual names readers could look up. Never use placeholder phrasing like "various local spots" or "a popular venue downtown."
For topics that are regional or national in nature — health conditions, medical topics, national trends, technology, sports leagues, or anything not tied to a specific Phoenix business or venue — do NOT force local business names into the article. You may briefly note Phoenix or Arizona context where it is genuinely relevant, but do not manufacture a local angle that was not in the source material or editor notes.
If the editor notes or PRIMARY SOURCE MATERIAL include a specific location, business, or local angle, always follow that — the editor's context overrides this rule in both directions.
The piece should read like it was written by a knowledgeable writer who covers Phoenix — not like every topic was artificially localized.
